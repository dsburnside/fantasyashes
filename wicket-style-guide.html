06 — Tone of Voice

Say It Short

Active voice, plain verbs, no filler. The app narrates the match — it doesn't apologise or hedge.

Avoid
Use instead
"Oops! Something went wrong while saving your team."
"Team not saved. Check your connection and retry."
"You have successfully submitted your entry!"
"Entry locked."
"No contests are currently available."
"No live contests. Check back at toss."
"Please select a captain to continue."
"Pick a captain to lock your XI."
07 — Motion

Feedback, Not Decoration

Motion exists to confirm live state — it never runs just to look alive.

Score tick
Points count up digit-by-digit on update. 400ms ease-out
Live dot
Soft pulse only while a match is in play. 1.8s loop
Transfer
Outgoing player slides left and fades; incoming slides in from right. 250ms
Card tap
Scale to 0.97 on press, spring back on release. 120ms
Reduced motion
All of the above collapse to instant state changes when the OS setting is on.
