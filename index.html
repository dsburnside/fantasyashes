The Fantasy Ashes

How it works

One squad per series, tied to your account — pick a team in My Squads, no league required. Leagues (create or join under My Leagues) are an optional way to compare your team against friends; join several on the same series and it's the same squad on every one of their leaderboards. Your job is to manage it well as team news and form change.

Squad rules

  1. 14-man squad, picked once at the start of the series from the two contesting teams' pools.
  2. At least 5 players from each team — no lopsided raids on either pool.
  3. At least one wicketkeeper in your squad, and your starting XI must always include one.
  4. No budget cap — pick on judgement, not salary.
  5. From your 14, name a starting XI — the other 3 sit on the bench. Free to change anytime in My Squads.
  6. Each player in your XI is also assigned a playing role — Batter, Bowler or Wicketkeeper — independent of their real-life role (so a keeper can be assigned as a Batter, an all-rounder as a Bowler, and so on). It doubles that discipline's bonus points: 50s/100s for a Batter, 4-/5-wicket hauls for a Bowler, catches/stumpings/run-outs for a Wicketkeeper. Set it from a player's card in My Squads, defaulting to their own base role (all-rounders default to Batter).
  7. Choose a Captain (2× points) and Vice-Captain from your XI. The Vice-Captain carries no bonus of their own — it only kicks in (2×) if your Captain doesn't play that Test.

Between Tests

  1. Your starting XI (11 of your 14) can be changed freely in My Squads at any time — it never counts as a transfer.
  2. Transfers — swapping a player out of your 14 for anyone else in the pool — are limited to 2 per Test, measured against your last locked squad. There's no limit at all before your first Test locks — chop and change your initial squad as much as you like right up to that first deadline. Committing just declares your intended squad; it's never blocked by the transfer count, but going over 2 (once the limit applies) without your wildcard is against the rules, so keep an eye on the count shown in My Squads before each deadline.
  3. Once per series, arm your Wildcard to lift that 2-transfer limit. It's only actually spent if you commit while it's armed and your squad is still locked in that way when the deadline passes — arming and backing out costs nothing.
  4. Selection locks at 10:00am local time on the morning of Day 1 of each Test — see the countdown at the top of the page. Whatever you last committed becomes your new baseline for the next round of transfers.
  5. Captain / Vice-Captain can be reassigned anytime among your current XI, free of charge.

Scoring

Batting
Per run+1
50 scored (2× if playing as Batter)+8
100 scored (replaces 50 bonus; 2× if playing as Batter)+16
Dismissed for a duck−2
Bowling & fielding
Per wicket+20
4-wicket haul (2× if playing as Bowler)+8
5-wicket haul (replaces 4-fer bonus; 2× if playing as Bowler)+16
Per catch (2× if playing as Wicketkeeper)+8
Per stumping (2× if playing as Wicketkeeper)+12
Per run-out (2× if playing as Wicketkeeper)+8

Each XI player's assigned playing role (Batter/Bowler/Wicketkeeper, set in My Squads) doubles that discipline's bonus points as marked above — independent of their real-life role. Separately, Captain multiplies their total match points by 2×. Vice-Captain gets no bonus unless the Captain doesn't play that Test — then the armband passes to them for 2× instead.
